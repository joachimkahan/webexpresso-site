Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Are the questionnaire and the explainer video really free?

Yes. The questionnaire is completed online, in about twenty minutes. You then receive, in under seven days, a free explainer video of your website.

No payment method is requested at this stage. You only pay if you order a plan.

What happens if the site does not suit me?

You pay nothing. The questionnaire and the explainer video are free: you only pay if you decide to order.

Is any computer knowledge required?

No. You answer questions about your business, and we take care of the rest, right up to putting the site online. If a question stops you, write to us.

Pricing and commitment

What is the total cost?

With the Essential plan: €200, paid once. With a subscription: €200 then €30 per month, or €250 then €50 per month, for at least 3 months. The minimum total is stated before you pay.

The address of your website is additional: €10 to €15 per year, in your name.

What are the differences between the three plans?
  • Essential (€200, paid once): your site, put online. No pro space and no subscription.
  • Autonomous (€200 then €30 per month): you edit your texts and photos yourself from your pro space. Each month, we handle 2 small requests for you, and 1 larger addition every 3 months.
  • Autonomous + support (€250 then €50 per month): the pro space, and an adviser who helps you build your visibility.

Compare the three plans

How does the 3-month commitment work?

It only applies to the two plans with a subscription. The minimum total, €290 or €400 depending on the plan, is stated before you pay, and you accept it by ticking a box.

If you stop before the end of the 3 months, the balance of those 3 months remains due. After that, you may stop whenever you wish, at no cost.

See what happens if you stop

Do I have to renew the subscription myself?

No. The monthly payment is taken automatically from your bank card each month. The subscription continues until you decide to end it.

How do I cancel the subscription?

With a simple email to contact@webexpresso.fr. We confirm the cancellation in writing. No notice period is required: the current month is due, then no further payment is taken.

During the first 3 months, the remaining monthly payments of the commitment are still due.

The Autonomous plan in detail

Why are your prices so low?

WebExpresso has no rent and no intermediary to pay: none of these costs is passed on in our prices. What you pay goes into making your website.

Your website belongs to you

Does the site really belong to me?

Yes. You always keep your website if you stop. The address of the site and its hosting are in your name.

With the Essential plan, there is never a subscription: you pay once.

What happens to my site if I stop the subscription?

Your site stays online, just as it is. After the first 3 months, stopping is free of charge.

If you stop before 12 months of subscription, you no longer have the pro space: to change a text or a photo, you make a request to us. If you resume later, the months already paid are counted.

After 12 months of subscription, even spread over several periods, the pro space is yours for good, with no monthly fee.

Is the pro space available without a subscription?

Prefer the pro space from the start, with no subscription? Available on request: €500.

After going online

What happens if there is a problem with my site?

You write to us. We investigate the origin of the problem, free of charge. If it stems from how the site was made, the repair is free for one year.

Read about the guarantees and repairs (in French)

How do I change a text, opening hours or a photo later on?

With a subscription, you do it yourself in the pro space, at any time.

With the Essential plan, you make a request to us: €20 to change a text or an image, €35 to add a simple block to a page. The price is given to you in advance.
