Before you start

Are the questionnaire and the preview really free?

Yes. You answer the questionnaire online, in about ten minutes. In under seven days, you receive a first preview of your website.

You are not asked for any payment method before that. You only pay if you order a plan.

What if I don't like the site?

You pay nothing. The questionnaire and the preview are free: you only pay if you decide to order.

I know nothing about computers. Is that a problem?

No. You answer simple questions, and we take care of the rest, right up to putting the site online. If a question stops you, write to us.

The price and the commitment

How much does it cost in total?

With the Essential plan: €200, once. With a subscription: €200 then €30 per month, or €250 then €50 per month, for at least 3 months. The minimum total is written down before you pay.

On top of that, there is the address of your website: €10 to €15 per year, in your name.

What is the difference between the three plans?
  • Essential (€200, one time only): your site, put online. No private space, no subscription.
  • Autonomous + updates (€200 then €30 per month): you change your texts and photos yourself in your private space (the pro space). Each month, we do 2 small requests for you, and 1 bigger addition every 3 months.
  • Autonomous + support (€250 then €50 per month): the pro space, and an adviser who helps you get known (business and marketing support). No promise of results.

Compare the three plans

How does the 3-month commitment work?

It only applies to the two plans with a subscription. The minimum total, €290 or €400 depending on the plan, is written down before you pay, and you tick a box to accept it.

If you stop before the end of the 3 months, you still owe what remains to be paid of those 3 months. After that, you stop whenever you like, at no cost.

Why are your prices so low?

We are students, at an engineering school and at a business school. We run WebExpresso alongside our studies, with no offices and no employees to pay.

Your website belongs to you

Is the site really mine?

Yes. You always keep your website if you stop. The address of the site and its hosting are in your name.

With the Essential plan, there is no subscription from the start.

What happens to my site if I stop the subscription?

Your site stays online, just as it is. After the first 3 months, stopping costs nothing.

If you stop before 12 months of subscription, you no longer have the pro space: to change a text or a photo, you go through us. If you take it up again later, the months you have already paid count.

After 12 months of subscription, even spread over several periods, the pro space stays yours for good, without paying anything each month.

Can I have the pro space without a subscription?

Prefer the pro space from the start, with no subscription? Available on request: €500.

After going online

What happens if my site has a problem?

You write to us. We look for where the problem comes from, free of charge. If it comes from how the site was made, the repair is free for one year.

Read about the guarantees and repairs (in French)

What if I want to change a text, my opening hours or a photo later?

With a subscription, you do it yourself in the pro space, whenever you like.

With the Essential plan, you ask us: €20 to change a text or an image, €35 to add a simple block to a page. You know the price beforehand.
