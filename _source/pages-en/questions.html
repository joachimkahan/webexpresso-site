Before you start

Are the questionnaire and the explainer video really free?

Yes. You answer the questionnaire online, in about twenty minutes. In under seven days, you receive a free explainer video: it presents your website before you pay for it.

You are not asked for any payment method before that. You only pay if you order a plan.

What if I don't like the site? Do I have to pay?

No, you pay nothing. The questionnaire and the explainer video are free: you only pay if you decide to order.

I know nothing about computers: is that a problem?

No. You answer simple questions, and we take care of the rest, right up to the point where your site is visible on the Internet. If a question stops you, write to us.

The price and the commitment

How much does it cost in total?

It depends on the plan. Essential: €200, once. With a subscription: €200 then €30 per month, or €250 then €50 per month, for at least 3 months. The minimum total is written down before you pay.

You also need to count the address of your website (for example www.your-name.com): €10 to €15 per year, in your name.

What is the difference between the three plans?
  • Essential (€200, one time only): your site, put online. No pro area, no subscription.
  • Autonomous + updates (€200 then €30 per month): you change your texts and photos yourself in your pro area, a private space that is yours alone. Each month, we carry out 2 small requests from you, and 1 bigger addition every 3 months.
  • Autonomous + support (€250 then €50 per month): the pro area, and an adviser who helps you get known (business and marketing support).

Compare the three plans

Am I committed if I take a subscription?

Yes, for at least 3 months, but only with the two plans with a subscription. The minimum total, €290 or €400 depending on the plan, is written down before you pay, and you tick a box to accept it.

If you stop before the end of the 3 months, you still pay the remaining months. After that, you stop whenever you like, at no cost.

See what happens if you stop

Why are your prices so low?

Because WebExpresso has no rent and no go-between to pay: nothing is added to your prices. What you pay goes into making your website.

Your website belongs to you

Is the site really mine?

Yes. You always keep your website if you stop. The address of the site and the place where it is stored on the Internet (the hosting) are in your name.

With the Essential plan, there is no subscription from the start.

What happens to my site if I stop the subscription?

It stays online, just as it is. After the first 3 months, stopping costs nothing.

If you stop before 12 months of subscription, you no longer have the pro area: to change a text or a photo, you go through us. If you take it up again later, the months you have already paid count.

If you have paid 12 months in total, even over several periods, the pro area stays yours for good, with no monthly payment.

Can I have the pro area without a subscription?

Prefer the pro area from the start, with no subscription? Available on request: €500.

After going online

What happens if my site has a problem?

You write to us, and we look for where the problem comes from, free of charge. If it comes from the way the site was made, the repair is free for one year.

Read about the guarantees and repairs (in French)

Will I be able to change a text, my opening hours or a photo later?

Yes. With a subscription, you do it yourself in the pro area, whenever you like.

With the Essential plan, you ask us: €20 to change a text or an image, €35 to add a simple block to a page. You know the price beforehand.
