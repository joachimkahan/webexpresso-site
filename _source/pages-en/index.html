Frequently asked questions

The essential answers are here; the others are covered on their own page.

Read all the questions
What if I don't like the site?

You pay nothing. The questionnaire and the explainer video are free: you only pay if you decide to order.

What is the total cost?

With the Essential plan: €200, once. With a subscription: €200 then €30 per month, or €250 then €50 per month, for at least 3 months. The minimum total is stated before payment.

On top of that, there is the address of your website: €10 to €15 per year, in your name.

Is the site really mine?

Yes. You always keep your website if you stop. The address of the site and its hosting are in your name.

Do I need any computer knowledge?

No. You answer questions about your business, and we take care of the rest, right up to putting the site online. If a question stops you, write to us.
