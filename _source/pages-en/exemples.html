Your own object, from your photos

Do you make or sell a piece of your own?

  1. You send us at least 3 photos of your piece, taken from different angles: front, side and top.
  2. We build its 3D version with artificial intelligence software.
  3. You approve it before it appears on your website.

Glass and very shiny or very thin objects do not suit this method: we will tell you frankly.
