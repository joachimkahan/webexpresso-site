On your website, an object can be turned with a fingertip, as if you were holding it. This cup is an example: you can spin it and change its colour.

{{inclure:scene-tasse}}

  • An object from your trade

    A loaf, a vase, a knife, an armchair: together we choose the object that speaks for your work, and we put it in 3D on your website.

  • In your website's colours

    Its colour, material and lighting match the rest of your website.

  • Your own object, from your photos

    Do you make or sell a piece of your own? Send us at least 3 photos taken from different angles: front, side and top. We recreate it in 3D using artificial intelligence, and you approve it before it goes online.

Glass and very shiny or very thin objects do not work well: we will tell you frankly.
