About us

WebExpresso builds websites for the self-employed, freelancers, tradespeople and small businesses all over France, from Grenoble. Every site is designed and built by Joachim, an engineering student at Grenoble INP.

The person who builds your website

Joachim designs every WebExpresso website, from the first mock-up to going online, then looks after it over time: your change requests, small jobs, your questions.

His engineering training shows in the work: fast websites, easy to read on a phone as well as a computer, finished down to the details, and commitments set out in writing.

Our conviction: a self-employed person, a tradesperson or a small business should be able to have a carefully made website without committing a large budget.
