What if I stop?

You always keep your website if you stop. For the two plans with a subscription, here is what changes depending on when you stop.

Before 3 months

The first 3 months are due

If you stop during the first 3 months, you still owe what remains to be paid of those 3 months. That is the minimum commitment: €290 or €400 in total, depending on the plan.

Between 3 and 12 months

You stop at no cost

Your site stays online, just as it is. You no longer have the pro space: to change a text or a photo, you go through us, one job at a time. If you take the subscription up again later, the months you have already paid count.

After 12 months of subscription

The pro space stays yours

The 12 months can be spread over several periods. You then keep the pro space for good, without paying anything each month. If you stop the subscription, additions made by us are paid job by job.
