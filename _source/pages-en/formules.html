The Autonomous plan, in detail

Autonomous is the plan we recommend to most of our clients. Here is what it offers compared with Essential, how it is paid and how to end it.

Why Autonomous rather than Essential

With Essential, your website is delivered as it is. Every change goes through us and is paid for individually: €20 to change a text or an image, €35 to add a section to a page.

With Autonomous, for €30 per month:

  • you edit your texts, your services and your images yourself from the pro space, as often as you need, at no extra cost;
  • we carry out 2 small requests for you each month, and 1 larger addition every 3 months;
  • after 12 months of uninterrupted subscription, the pro space remains available to you with no monthly payment.

Two changes ordered at the individual rate, at €20 each, already cost more than one monthly payment. As soon as your website needs updating at least twice a month (prices, opening hours, services, new work), Autonomous costs less than Essential.

Payment: automatic, every month

You pay €200 to build the website, then €30 per month. The monthly payment is taken automatically from your bank card by our secure payment service. You have nothing to renew: the subscription continues month after month until you decide to end it. If a payment is declined and not settled within 30 days, the subscription ends.

Cancelling: online or by email

You cancel online, from the page Cancel a subscription (in French), or by simple email to contact@webexpresso.fr. We confirm the cancellation in writing. No notice period is required: the current monthly period, which runs from date to date since you subscribed, is due, then no further payment is taken. The pro space remains available until the end of that last period.

The only condition is the 3-month commitment. If you cancel during the first 3 months, the remaining monthly payments up to the end of the third month are still due.

What you keep

Your website belongs to you in every case: it stays online if you stop. Before 12 months of subscription, stopping ends your access to the pro space, and changes are then charged individually. After 12 months of uninterrupted subscription, the pro space remains available to you with no monthly payment, for as long as WebExpresso provides this service: you keep editing your website yourself.

These payment and cancellation rules also apply to the Autonomous + support plan.
