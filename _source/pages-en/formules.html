If you stop

You always keep your website if you stop. For the two plans with a subscription, the terms depend on when you stop.

Before 3 months

The first 3 months are due

If you stop during the first 3 months, the balance of those 3 months remains payable. That is the minimum commitment: €290 or €400 in total, depending on the plan.

Between 3 and 12 months

You stop at no cost

Your site stays online as it is. You no longer have the pro space: to change a text or a photo, you send us a request, charged job by job. If you take the subscription up again later, the months you have already paid count.

After 12 months of subscription

The pro space stays yours

The 12 months can be spread over several periods. You then keep the pro space permanently, without paying anything each month. If you stop the subscription, additions made by us are charged job by job.
