How it works

Four steps, from your answers to your website online. The first two are free. You only pay at the third, and only if you like the site.

{{inclure:picto-tasse}}

1. You answer the questionnaire

Free

What you do

You answer about twenty simple questions about your business, online. Allow about ten minutes.

See what the questionnaire asks you

What we do

We read your answers. Where you hesitated, or answered « Advise me », we talk it over with you.

What it costs

€0. The questionnaire is free and commits you to nothing. You are not asked for any payment method.

{{inclure:picto-calendrier}}

2. We prepare your website

Free

What you do

Nothing. You wait for our message: we write to you at the e-mail address you gave in the questionnaire.

What we do

We build a first preview of your website, from your answers. You receive it in under seven days.

What it costs

€0. The preview is free too. You only pay if you decide to order.

{{inclure:picto-ticket}}

3. You choose your plan

From €200

What you do

You look at the preview. If you like it, you choose one of the three plans. If you do not like it, you pay nothing.

What we do

We write down the total to pay before you pay. For a subscription, you tick a box to accept the 3-month commitment. You pay by bank card, on a secure page.

What it costs

From €200. Depending on the plan:

  • Essential: €200, one time only. No subscription.
  • Autonomous + updates: €200 + €30 per month, minimum commitment of 3 months, so €290 at least.
  • Autonomous + support: €250 + €50 per month, minimum commitment of 3 months, so €400 at least.

Compare the three plans

{{inclure:picto-cle}}

4. We put your website online

Ready to use

What you do

Nothing technical. The address of your website (for example www.your-name.com) and its hosting, which is the place it takes up on the Internet, are in your name: they belong to you.

What we do

We put your website online and take care of everything. Your customers can view it on a phone as well as on a computer.

What it costs

€10 to €15 per year for the address of your website. Putting it online is included in your plan.

What the questionnaire asks you

About twenty questions, in six parts. You answer in your own words, the way you would talk about your trade to a customer.

  • You and your business. Your name, your trade, your town, and whether you already have a website.
  • Your customers. Who you work for, why you want a website, and what visitors should do first: call you, ask for a quote, come to the shop.
  • Your offer. What you provide, your prices if you want to show them, and what makes people choose you.
  • The content of the site. What you can give us (photos of your work, reviews from real customers, opening hours) and the plan you are considering. You can answer « I don't know yet ».
  • The mood. The feeling the site should give, a rather light or rather dark background, your logo and your colours if you have them.
  • Your contact details and legal information. Phone, e-mail, address, SIRET number: what the law requires on any business website.

Good to know before you start

Not sure about a question? Leave the answer empty, or choose « Advise me »: we talk it over together.

Short of time? Your answers stay on your device until you send them. You can close the page and come back later, on the same device.

Nothing to pay. The questionnaire does not ask you for any payment method.

And then?

Your website is online, and it is yours. You always keep your website if you stop.

Depending on your plan

With a subscription, you change your texts and photos yourself in your private space (the Pro Space). With the Essential plan, the site stays online just as it is, with nothing to pay each month.

See what each plan includes

Repairs and small jobs

Without a subscription, you ask us for a touch-up when you need one. Changing a text or a photo costs €20. Adding a simple section to your site costs €35. The diagnosis is free, and a manufacturing defect of the site is fixed at no cost for one year.
