About us

Students. We study at an engineering school, Grenoble INP, and at a business school. WebExpresso is the project we run alongside our studies. We build websites for the self-employed, freelancers, tradespeople and small businesses, from Grenoble.

Two schools, one project

Some of us learn to build websites, at the engineering school. The others learn to listen to and advise customers, at the business school. WebExpresso brings the two together.

This project teaches us our future trade on real websites, for real people. It is one more reason to take care over each one.

Our starting idea is simple: a craftsperson, a shopkeeper or a self-employed person should be able to have a real, well-made website without spending a large budget.

What we commit to

Read the guarantees in detail (in French)
  • The preview is free. You see your website before any payment. If you do not like it, you pay nothing.
  • You always keep your website if you stop. The address of the site and its hosting are in your name.
  • The total is written down before you pay. For a subscription, the minimum total is written down too: €290 or €400 depending on the plan, for the 3-month commitment.
  • No promise we cannot keep. We do not guarantee a number of customers, or a place on Google.
  • A one-year guarantee. A fault in the way we built your site is fixed at no cost for one year.
  • The diagnosis is free. If your site has a problem, we look for where it comes from without charging you anything.
