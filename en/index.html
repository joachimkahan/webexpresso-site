Read all the questions
What if I don't like the site?

You pay nothing. The questionnaire and the preview are free: you only pay if you decide to order.

How much does it cost in total?

With the Essential plan: €200, once. With a subscription: €200 then €30 per month, or €250 then €50 per month, for at least 3 months. The minimum total is written down before you pay.

On top of that, there is the address of your website: €10 to €15 per year, in your name.

Is the site really mine?

Yes. You always keep your website if you stop. The address of the site and its hosting are in your name.

I know nothing about computers. Is that a problem?

No. You answer simple questions, and we take care of the rest, right up to putting the site online. If a question stops you, write to us.

Shall we start? It is free, and it commits you to nothing.

Ten minutes of simple questions about your business. We then prepare your website and show it to you. You decide after seeing it.
