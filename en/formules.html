What if I stop?

You always keep your website if you stop. For the two plans with a subscription, here is what changes depending on when you stop.

Before 3 months

The first 3 months are due

If you stop during the first 3 months, you still owe what remains to be paid of those 3 months. That is the minimum commitment: €290 or €400 in total, depending on the plan.

Between 3 and 12 months

You stop at no cost

Your site stays online, just as it is. You no longer have the pro space: to change a text or a photo, you go through us, one job at a time. If you take the subscription up again later, the months you have already paid count.

After 12 months of subscription

The pro space stays yours

The 12 months can be spread over several periods. You then keep the pro space for good, without paying anything each month. If you stop the subscription, additions made by us are paid job by job.

What you pay for separately

The address of your website

For example www.your-name.com. It costs €10 to €15 per year. It is in your name, and you are the one who pays for it.

The hosting of the site

This is the place where your site is kept on the Internet. It is in your name, with GitHub, the service we use for all our sites. Like the address, it belongs to you.

Payment

By bank card

You pay on a secure page, the one of Stripe, an online payment service. You only pay when you order: the questionnaire and the preview are free.

The total written down before you pay

Before paying, you read the total: €200, or the minimum of €290 or €400 for a subscription. For a subscription, you also tick a box to accept the 3-month commitment.

After going online: repairs and small jobs

Is something wrong? We look for where it comes from, free of charge. If it comes from how the site was made, the repair is free for one year.

For everything else, prices are fixed and you know them in advance.
